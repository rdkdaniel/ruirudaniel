A LITTLE ABOUT ME

About

An academic profile connecting research, teaching and practical applications of intelligent systems.

Researching intelligent decision-making

I am a doctoral researcher at Université Paris 8 (France) and Strathmore University (Kenya), working on Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL) for resource allocation problems. My research explores how learning agents can make more effective decisions by improving how experiences are represented, selected and used during training.

Alongside my doctoral work, I am a financial engineer who manages a personal investment portfolio, with a long-term goal of growing it steadily. I am also a businessperson involved in a variety of sectors in Kenya. In addition, I teach in the fields of Engineering and Computer Science at Strathmore University.

WHAT I WORK ON

Research interests

Core themes shaping my doctoral research and broader interests in intelligent systems.

I am working to make this section more interactive and exploratory. For now, the topics below are presented as static headlines.

Deep Reinforcement Learning

Value-based and policy-based learning methods for sequential decision-making in complex environments.

Resource Allocation

Learning-based approaches to allocating limited resources under dynamic conditions and competing objectives.

Prioritised Experience Learning

Experience replay, clustering and prioritisation strategies to improve the learning process and decision quality.

Transformer-enhanced RL

Exploring attention-based representations and transformer architectures within deep reinforcement learning agents.

Evaluation and Generalisation

Benchmarking agent performance, transfer to new environments and evaluating robustness across tasks.

AI for Language and Applications

Interest in applying learning-based AI methods to language technologies and practical application domains.
